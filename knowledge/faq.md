# Prana Path help centre

Prana Path is a fictional Indian wellness brand used in this demo. Every name, price, address, and policy in this file is made up.

## Programs and prices

Prana Path sells three self-paced programs. Each one is a one-time payment in Indian rupees with lifetime access; there is no subscription.

- 21-Day Breathwork Reset: ₹1,499. Twenty-one guided breathing sessions of 10 to 15 minutes, one per day.
- Sleep Better Audio Pack: ₹799. Fourteen wind-down audio tracks and a printable sleep journal.
- Desk Yoga Video Course: ₹999. Thirty short stretching videos you can follow at your desk.

Prices include GST. We do not offer discount codes or payment in instalments.

## Getting access after you pay

After a successful payment, an access email from hello@pranapath.example reaches you within 15 minutes. Your programs live in the Member Library at learn.pranapath.example; sign in with the email address you used at checkout. One purchase works on up to 3 devices.

If the access email has not arrived after 15 minutes, check your spam folder, then message us with the phone number or email address you paid with.

## Payments

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking through Razorpay. Prana Path never sees or stores your card number.

- Failed payment: if money left your account but the payment shows as failed, your bank reverses the debit automatically within 5 to 7 working days. You do not need to ask for a refund of a failed payment.
- Charged twice: if two payments succeeded for the same order, we refund the extra payment in full, whatever its date. Message us and we will raise it with the team.

Prana Path will never ask for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, or passwords, in chat or on a call.

## Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 11 days of purchase, counted from the payment date. Message us and we will open a refund ticket for you.

- Our team reviews each refund request within 2 working days.
- Once a refund is approved, the money returns to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
- Access to the program is removed when the refund is processed.
- After 11 days we cannot refund a purchase, except for a double charge.

## Support hours and contact

Our support team works from 10:00 to 18:30 IST, Monday to Saturday, and is closed on Sundays and national holidays. The assistant answers at any time; messages for the team that arrive outside these hours are answered on the next working day.

You can also email the team at help@pranapath.example.
